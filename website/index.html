Sensor Stack

Multi-Modal Detection

Each node combines three sensors for redundant fire detection. Rate-of-change triggers with multi-node statistical consensus reduce false positives.

PMS5003

Primary Smoke Channel
  • Principle: Laser scattering
  • Output: PM1.0, PM2.5, PM10
  • Range: 0.3–10 µm
  • Interface: UART (9600 baud)
  • Power: 5 V / ~100 mA peak

BME680

Fire-Weather + Secondary VOC
  • Temperature: –40 to 85 °C (±1.0 °C)
  • Humidity: 0–100 % RH (±3 %)
  • Pressure: 300–1100 hPa (±0.6 hPa)
  • Gas (VOC): MOX sensor, IAQ index
  • Interface: I²C / SPI

AS3935

Lightning Detection
  • Range: Up to 40 km
  • Sensitivity: Programmable threshold
  • Noise rejection: Built-in algorithm
  • Interface: I²C / SPI
  • Power: 3.3 V / &lt;50 µA listening
Power System

Autonomous Energy

18650 Li-ion Cell

Chemistry: Li-ion (NMC or LiFePO4 option)

Capacity: 3000–3500 mAh typical

Nominal: 3.7 V / 3.2 V (LiFePO4)

Runtime: 7–14 days no sun (duty-cycled)

Protection: On-board BMS (over/under-voltage, current)

5 V / 2 W Solar Panel

Voltage: 5 V regulated output

Power: 2 W (peak, full sun)

Charge current: ~350 mA max into cell

Mounting: Adjustable tilt, pole or tree strap

Weatherproof: ETFE laminate, IP67

Cold-Charge Gate (Hardware)

Cut-off: ~0 °C (charging disabled)

Re-enable: ~2.3 °C (hysteresis)

Implementation: Discrete MOSFET + temp sensor

No MCU required: Fully autonomous

Protects: Cell from lithium plating at low temp

How It Detects & What It Shares

Prototype

Detection Radius & Logic

Each node covers approximately 100 m effective radius in typical forest terrain. Detection uses rate-of-change on PM2.5 and temperature, combined with lightning proximity from the AS3935. Multi-node statistical consensus (requiring correlated triggers across ≥2 nodes within a time window) rejects single-node false positives from dust, cooking smoke, or sensor glitches.

Prototype

Privacy by Design

Only environmental telemetry leaves the property: PM2.5, temperature, humidity, pressure, battery voltage. No cameras, no microphones, no GPS coordinates, no device identifiers beyond a random node ID. A local-only mode is available — the base station runs entirely on your LAN with Home Assistant / MQTT, no cloud connection, no away-from-home alerts.

About

Stephen Nordlund — Founder

Stephen Nordlund founded Nordtronics to build the off-grid power and sensing tools he wanted for his own property in Pagosa Springs, Colorado — a place where the grid ends and the forest begins.

Background: embedded firmware (ESP32, STM32, Nordic), power electronics (BMS design, solar charge controllers), and LoRa/802.15.4 mesh networks. Previously built telemetry systems for industrial and scientific applications.

Nordtronics is a one-person shop. Every board is laid out, every line of firmware written, every battery pack assembled here. No outside investors. No corporate roadmap. Just honest engineering for people who live off-grid.
