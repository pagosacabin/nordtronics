## Difficulty levels and their mapping

| level | candidates | Stockfish depth ceiling | time cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| easy | 3 | 8 | 0.25 s |
| normal | 4 | 12 | 0.45 s |
| hard | 5 | 16 | 0.80 s |

Depth is a ceiling, the time cap is the hard limit — whichever ends first. `medium` is
accepted as an alias of normal; missing or unrecognised values fall back to normal.
`personality` (`aggressive`, `teach me`, `default`) is allowlisted the same way. Both
travel in the request body when the page sends them; the page does not yet, so the
default is normal/default — Juno's follow-up.
