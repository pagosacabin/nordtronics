# 0225 — N16R8 probe (living frame board verification)

## Assignment

Hermes does this task. Firmware stays Hermes's (protocol 1.3.0). Copilot peer-reviews the committed probe sketch, routed by Hermes via Herdr like Newton; Copilot is a reviewer, not the proof — Juno verifies independently on top.

## Context

- The living frame's ship build is 0180: 640x480 at 16 colours, portrait-native, on the Heltec. Stephen's verdict: "The last test was the best test" — resolution beats colour depth on this DAC + panel.
- The N16R8 rung only has to add the 64 colours back at the already-approved 640x480 portrait resolution. It does NOT re-decide resolution, orientation, or the palette pipeline. This task is the probe that gates that port — it is not the port.
- Why the probe matters: the Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 V3 turned out to be an ESP32-S3FN8 with NO PSRAM — probe confirmed 0 bytes — although Heltec ships the V3 with both S3R2 and S3FN8 chips under the same label. A model name is not a measurement.
- The board: one of the six ESP32-S3 N16R8 devkits ($35.97, ordered Nov 12, arrived Nov 20). Stephen identified it from the silkscreen as a YD-ESP32-23 2022-V1.3 (VCC-GND); the module can is marked S3-N16R8. Listing claims 16 MB flash, 8 MB octal PSRAM. Clone caveats apply: silkscreen pin labels can lie, and the PSRAM must be proven real, not just reported.
- The arithmetic that makes PSRAM the gate: 0180's 4 bpp framebuffer is 153,600 B in internal RAM (free heap after init 209,524 B, largest block 167,936 B). A 64-colour 640x480 framebuffer is 8 bpp = 307,200 B — it does not fit in internal RAM. No real PSRAM, no 64-colour rung.
- Stephen wired the board himself on Nov 20: the VGA breakout is wired to the DAC pins and the SD card holder is wired on SPI, per the pin map below (agreed with Stephen before wiring). An SD card is inserted in the holder — it just came in, and its status, format, and contents are UNKNOWN.

## Agreed pin map (Stephen wired to this on Nov 20)

VGA DAC:
- Red: GPIO 4, 5
- Green: GPIO 6, 7
- Blue: GPIO 15, 16
- HSYNC: GPIO 17
- VSYNC: GPIO 18

SD holder (SPI):
- SCK: GPIO 12
- MOSI: GPIO 11
- MISO: GPIO 13
- CS: GPIO 10
- GPIO 14: spare (card-detect if the holder exposes it)

Warning: on an N16R8 module, GPIO 35, 36, and 37 are the octal PSRAM bus lines inside the module — they are silkscreened on this board but are NOT usable GPIO. Nothing is wired to them; keep it that way.

## Task

Probe the ONE board Stephen wired. Leave the other five boards alone.

1. Identify the board: chip model, flash size, and PSRAM detection, from esptool / the probe firmware's own reports — not from the listing, the silkscreen, or the module can.
2. Report PSRAM size as the firmware sees it, and what type the IDF/Arduino APIs report (octal vs quad) if they report one.
3. Prove the PSRAM is real: write a known pattern across the FULL claimed 8 MB, read it back, and report pass/fail with mismatch count. A size report alone does not prove it — clones can lie about size.
4. Check the silkscreen on the wired pins: verify that each labeled GPIO in the agreed pin map (the 8 DAC pins and the 4 SD pins) actually is the GPIO it claims — e.g. by toggling each in turn — and list every discrepancy, or state "none found." Do not trust the labels.
5. SD card, READ-ONLY: on the agreed SPI pins, attempt to mount the inserted card and report what you find — filesystem type, capacity, and a top-level listing if it mounts; or the exact failure mode if it does not. Do NOT format, erase, or write to the card in this task — its contents are unknown and it is Stephen's call what happens to it.
6. Do NOT start the 64-colour port in this task, and do not drive the display beyond what the pin check needs. That is the next rung, after the probe passes.

## Success criteria

- Reply names: chip model, flash size, PSRAM size in bytes as measured, PSRAM type if reported.
- Full-8 MB pattern test result: pass, or fail with mismatch count and the address range that failed.
- Pin-label check on the 12 wired pins: discrepancies listed, or "none found."
- SD report: mounted (filesystem, capacity) or the specific failure — with no writes made to the card.
- Probe sketch committed in the repo (see Proof).

## Constraints

- Probe the one wired board only. Do not flash over anything else, and do not reflash the Heltec's 0180 build.
- This task is a probe, not the port. If the PSRAM is fake, missing, or smaller than claimed, stop and report — do not work around it in this task.
- The SD card is read-only for this task: no format, no erase, no test writes.
- Cost: keep it a probe. No new purchases in this task.
