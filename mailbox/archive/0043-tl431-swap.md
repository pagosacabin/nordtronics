### 2. Pinout: why this swap does not touch the board

TI's pin tables, both SOT-23-3 (DBZ), and both agree with KiCad's symbols:

| Part | Datasheet | Pin 1 | Pin 2 | Pin 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TL431 (TI SLVS543S, Table 5-1) | | **CATHODE (K)** | **REF** | **ANODE (A)** |
| TLV431 (TI SLVS139Z, Table 4-1) | | **REF** | **CATHODE (K)** | **ANODE (A)** |

Pins 1 and 2 swap between the two parts — exactly the trap 0039 noted. It is
harmless here **only because `REF` is tied to `CATHODE` on this design** (0039
correction 3): both are on `REF2V5`, so pad 1 and pad 2 are the same node, and
pin 3 is `ANODE` on `GND` in both parts. The net-per-pad assignment is
therefore identical, and the netlist confirms it:

```
/GND:    D1/3  (A_3)                     previous: D1/3  (ANODE_3)
/REF2V5: D1/1  (K_1), D1/2 (REF_2),      previous: D1/1  (REF_1), D1/2 (CATHODE_2),
         C1/1, R1/1, R2/1, Rs/2                    C1/1, R1/1, R2/1, Rs/2
```

If REF and K were not tied together, this substitution would have required
swapping the copper on pads 1 and 2. On this board it does not.

### 7. Bias current: TL431 vs TLV431 on this branch (numbers only)

Datasheet numbers actually used:

| Parameter | TL431 (SLVS543S) | TLV431 (SLVS139Z) |
|---|---|---|
| V_REF | 2.440 / 2.495 / 2.550 V | 1.24 V |
| **Cathode current to regulate, I_K(min)** | **0.4 mA (min) / 1 mA (max)** | **55 uA (min) / 80 uA (max)** |
| Reference input current I_ref | 2 uA typ / 4 uA max | 0.15 uA typ / 0.5 uA max |

What the reference branch draws. `Rs` = 12k from `Panel+` to `REF2V5`:
`I_Rs = (Vpanel - 2.5 V) / 12k`. The node also feeds the R1/R2 dividers and
D1's own REF input:

| T | NTC | I(R1+TH1) | I(R2+R3) | I_ref | node load |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 °C | 33.6k | 39.3 uA | 41.7 uA | 2.0 uA | 83.0 uA |
| 25 °C | 10.0k | 62.5 uA | 41.7 uA | 2.0 uA | 106.2 uA |
| 40 °C | 5.30k | 70.8 uA | 41.7 uA | 2.0 uA | 114.5 uA |
| 50 °C | 3.59k | 74.4 uA | 41.7 uA | 2.0 uA | 118.1 uA |

The load is largest at the hot end, which is exactly when the gate is supposed
to be on — so that is the case that matters.

| Vpanel | I_Rs | I_K (0/25/40 °C) | shunt dissipation @2.5 V | branch draw |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 V | 0.208 mA | 0.125 / 0.102 / 0.094 mA | 0.23 mW | 1.0 mW |
| 8 V | 0.458 mA | 0.375 / 0.352 / 0.344 mA | 0.86 mW | 3.7 mW |
| 10 V | 0.625 mA | 0.542 / 0.519 / 0.511 mA | 1.28 mW | 6.2 mW |
| 12 V | 0.792 mA | 0.709 / 0.685 / 0.677 mA | 1.69 mW | 9.5 mW |
| 15 V | 1.042 mA | 0.959 / 0.935 / 0.927 mA | 2.32 mW | 15.6 mW |
| 18 V | 1.292 mA | 1.209 / 1.185 / 1.177 mA | 2.94 mW | 23.2 mW |
| 20 V | 1.458 mA | 1.375 / 1.352 / 1.344 mA | 3.36 mW | 29.2 mW |
| 24 V | 1.792 mA | 1.709 / 1.685 / 1.677 mA | 4.19 mW | 43.0 mW |

The currents above the shunt's needs are burned in the shunt either way: the
branch current is set by `Rs` and the panel, not by which reference is fitted.
What changes is the **floor** at which the reference works at all (40 °C,
114.5 uA node load):

| Requirement | Panel voltage needed |
|---|---|
| TL431, I_K = 1.0 mA (guaranteed max spec) | >= 15.9 V |
| TL431, I_K = 0.4 mA (typ min spec) | >= 8.7 V |
| TLV431, I_K = 80 uA (max spec) | >= 4.8 V |
| TLV431, I_K = 55 uA | >= 4.5 V |

So: at a 12 V panel the branch supplies 0.79 mA, leaving 0.68 mA through the
shunt — above the TL431's typical 0.4 mA but **below its guaranteed 1 mA
limit**, and the TLV431 it replaces would have needed ~4.8 V to do better than
the TL431 does at 15.9 V. The TL431's V_KA floor is also 2.5 V against the
TLV431's 1.24 V. In other words the swap buys the correct 2.5 V reference and
pays for it in minimum operating panel voltage and in a bias network that now
has to be sized for ~1 mA. Sizing `Rs` to guarantee 1 mA at a 12 V panel would
need `Rs <= 8.5k`, which raises the branch draw at 18 V from 1.29 mA to
1.82 mA. **Per the task I re-scaled nothing and changed no resistor** — these
are the numbers for Stephen's later decision.
