NEW FINDINGS THIS PASS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

LOW-1  server.py:407-408  do_OPTIONS 403 missing close+drain
  do_OPTIONS refuses the OPTIONS method before any body is sent (OPTIONS has
  no body under RFC 7231), so this is not a request-smuggling or
  slot-accounting defect. It is a consistency/hygiene gap: every other
  error path sets close_connection = True; this one leaves the connection
  open after a rejected preflight. A determined client could pipeline further
  OPTIONS requests on the same socket. Because OPTIONS carries no body the
  attack surface is negligible, and drain is unnecessary. Recommended fix:
    self.close_connection = True
    self._send(403, {"error": "origin not allowed"}, origin)
  (drain=False is fine).
