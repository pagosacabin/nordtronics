0280 -- live-photo colour A/B, measured on the bench 2026-11-27 20:0x-20:2x MST
===============================================================================

Camera board: Freenove ESP32-Wrover CAM (GC0308, pid 0x9b) on /dev/ttyUSB0 (CH340).
NOTE the port: this bench enumerates the camera on ttyUSB0 and the frame board's
FT232R on ttyUSB1, the reverse of what the freenove-cam README assumes (ttyUSB1).
Frame board (ttyUSB1) was not touched: no console is usable on it and this A/B does
not need it.

Firmware under test: hermes/0261-mirror-live-photo @ b5c0f525, freenove-cam,
plus a bench-only variant (not committed): -DWB_STRENGTH / -DSAT_LEVEL compile
knobs and a 'j' serial command that captures locally and dumps the exact
307,200-byte panel raster. See bench-variant.diff.

Per shot: reset, wait for boot + WiFi association, send 'j'. The firmware prints
the shoot line (with the WB gains it computed) then "raster  : BEGIN <len> <sha256>"
followed by the raw raster, then END. The host re-hashes the received bytes and
requires it to equal the firmware's own SHA; every capture below matched
(len 307,200, sha equal on both sides), so no byte was lost on the 115200 link.

Max per-channel level delta is 2 in every case; the 64-colour raster has only
4 levels per channel, so a mid-tone moves at most one quant step per channel.

What the numbers say, without guessing at Stephen's eye:
  1. Grey-world WB is doing almost all of the work. At the shipped 0.50 the blue
     channel is multiplied by 1.84 on this scene, i.e. a warm scene loses 46% of its
     red-over-blue ratio (2.16 with WB vs 5.54 without). Halving the strength keeps
     most of that back (R/B 3.02); zero gives the sensor's own warm cast.
  2. Saturation -1 -> 0 -> +1 at WB 0.50 changes nothing measurable: mean channel
     levels agree to three decimals and the pixel difference sits inside the
     same-config repeat band. Treat suspect 2 as inert on this GC0308 driver at
     this scale (report only; no fix applied).
  3. The scene is not under my control: I could not point the camera at a specific
     warm-lit subject (nobody at the bench). The board is aimed at whatever the
     fixed mount sees -- bright upper field, dark lower-left, warm-dominated by
     its own WB record. The ranking above is like-for-like on that fixed scene,
     so it is valid for the knob comparison, but "warmer" for Stephen's own scene
     still has to be his call from the candidates.

Reconstruction used for the PNGs (do not read the PNG as a photometric match to
the panel; it is the panel's intended signal):
  level v -> linear light L = (s_dac_v[ch][v]/fs)^2.2 using the 0250 measured
  ladder, then sRGB-encoded for a normal viewer. The raster itself is the exact
  byte stream the frame board writes to /sdcard/LIVE and clocks to the DAC.
